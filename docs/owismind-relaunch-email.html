For the curious
What is OWIsMind, exactly?
The idea
OWIsMind brings together LLM models, an agentic system and solid software engineering to get the best out of your data. You ask a question in plain language; it finds, computes and explains the answer.
Available today
The DRIVE project data: revenue (actuals, budget, HLF and S3F forecasts) and incident tickets.
Not your data? Come anyway
The goal is to extend OWIsMind to all teams, as a richer alternative to dashboards, which can often be limiting. See the capabilities now, and we can build agents on your own data, based on your needs.
